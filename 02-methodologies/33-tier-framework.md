# The 33-Tier Framework — Transferable Method

## Foundational sequence

**Point → Knowing → Witness → Gate**

- **Point:** Establish why the work exists: problem, intended outcome, scope and decision.
- **Knowing:** Separate established facts from assumptions, hypotheses, interpretations and unknowns.
- **Witness:** Require inspectable evidence with provenance, dates, methods, limitations and observation conditions.
- **Gate:** Define the conditions required before work or a claim advances. A gate is a decision boundary, not ceremonial approval.

## Core rules

1. Evidence precedes confidence.
2. A documented design is not an implementation.
3. An implementation is not proof of correct operation.
4. A passing test supports only the scope and conditions it exercised.
5. Verification does not automatically confer authority to proceed.
6. Unknowns remain unknown until evidence resolves them.
7. Contradictions are recorded and investigated, not hidden.
8. Significant claims retain traceable provenance.
9. Failed tests and limitations remain part of the evidence record.
10. Advancement requires the relevant gate conditions and ratification.

## Research workflow

`QUESTION → CONTRACT → QUERY PLAN → SOURCE FETCHING → EVIDENCE LEDGER → VERIFICATION → SOURCED REPORT`

A research contract establishes question, decision, as-of date, scope, comparisons, must-resolve issues, source rules, expected output and stop condition.

Independent query lanes reduce dependence on one source or framing. Prefer original sources. Search snippets and model recollection are leads, not evidence.

The evidence ledger should preserve claim ID, exact claim, status, source/date, relevant passage, provenance/independence, limitations, missing proof, verification action and outcome.

## Gate record

Every gate should state:
- what is evaluated;
- required evidence;
- what was actually inspected/tested;
- unresolved matters;
- ratification authority;
- decision and rationale.

A gate is not complete merely because documentation exists or implementation activity occurred.

## Important maturity boundary

The source document explicitly states that the detailed definitions of all 33 tiers, dependencies, entry/exit criteria, evidence requirements and validation method remain open. Do not claim that all 33 tiers are individually specified, validated, certified or universally applicable.

The framework is not a substitute for domain expertise, independent review, testing, law or applicable standards. Its effectiveness must be evaluated in practice.

## Adaptation rule

Use this framework as an evidence and decision discipline, not as a reason to add ceremony. Apply only the tiers or checks relevant to the question and risk. Record when a particular application is incomplete or unsuitable.
