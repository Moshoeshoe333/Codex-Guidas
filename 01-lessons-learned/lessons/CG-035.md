# CG-035 — Immutable claim IDs require an explicit revision/supersession model for legitimate updates and revocations.

**Source / origin:** ScoutSA OPA-016

## Reusable control

Separate stable subject identity from immutable event/revision identity. Preserve actor, time, source, revision and supersedes/revokes links; never turn a source claim into operational authority automatically.
