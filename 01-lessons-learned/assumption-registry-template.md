## Status definitions

- **Untested:** plausible but no relevant test has been run.
- **Testing:** a test is underway; outcome not yet known.
- **Supported:** evidence supports the statement within a defined scope; not universal proof.
- **Contradicted:** relevant evidence conflicts with the assumption.
- **Retired:** no longer needed for the decision or superseded by a new statement.

## Review questions

1. What do we currently hope is true?
2. What is the strongest evidence for it?
3. What is the strongest evidence against it?
4. Is the evidence independent, recent and representative?
5. What happens if we are wrong?
6. What is the smallest ethical test that could change our decision?
7. Does this assumption still justify spending time or money?

Do not use unsupported numeric confidence scores. If a qualitative confidence label is used, explain the evidence behind it.
