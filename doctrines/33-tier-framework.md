# The 33-Tier Epistemological Framework

**Status:** Foundational working doctrine; detailed tier taxonomy and validation remain incomplete.  
**Related method:** [33-Tier Framework — Transferable Method](../02-methodologies/33-tier-framework.md)  
**Source lineage:** SoulSync 3 framework; source archaeology dated 2026-11-18.

> **“The Point is the reason. The Knowing is the revelation of its application.”**

## 1. Purpose and axiom

The framework is our way of moving from a question or intention to a justified decision. It asks why a thing matters, what is actually known, what bears witness to that knowledge, and what conditions must be met before action is authorized.

Its epistemic sequence is:

**Point → Knowing → Witness → Gate**

- **Point:** the reason, problem, intended outcome and decision.
- **Knowing:** what is established, assumed, inferred, disputed or unknown.
- **Witness:** inspectable evidence, its provenance, method, date, limitations and independence.
- **Gate:** the explicit criteria and authorized decision required to advance.

The framework is intended to filter noise, not manufacture certainty. No process can guarantee “pure” or “undeniable” truth merely by applying more stages.

## 2. The 33-perspective filter

Before a consequential architecture, dependency, feature, research conclusion or business model is adopted, examine it through up to 33 relevant perspectives.

Examples include:
- maintainability and simplicity;
- developer cognitive load and handover;
- hardware, bandwidth, power and data consumption;
- total financial and operational cost;
- user dignity, consent and autonomy;
- privacy and data minimisation;
- accessibility and inclusion;
- safeguarding and abuse resistance;
- security and threat exposure;
- failure modes and blast radius;
- resilience, recovery and reversibility;
- interoperability and dependency risk;
- observability and testability;
- performance and scalability;
- legal and regulatory obligations;
- local context and cultural/linguistic fit;
- support burden and long-term viability.

**Important:** these are examples, not the completed canonical list of 33. The full 33 lenses, definitions, weights (if any), evidence requirements, entry/exit criteria and validation method remain to be specified and tested. Do not invent missing lenses to make a checklist look complete.

Use proportionality: apply deeper scrutiny where consequence, uncertainty or irreversibility is high. Record lenses not assessed and why. Do not turn every small change into a 33-item ceremony.

## 3. The 99-tier sieve — three passes, not false precision

For high-volume or high-stakes information, the 99-tier sieve applies the 33-perspective method in three distinct passes:

1. **Pass A — Decompose (perspectives 1–33):** identify claims, entities, dates, sources, assumptions, contradictions, missing data and possible manipulation/noise.
2. **Pass B — Cross-examine (perspectives 34–66):** compare claims against independent sources, project history, technical constraints, domain expertise, failure modes and counter-evidence.
3. **Pass C — Synthesize (perspectives 67–99):** state what is supported, what remains uncertain, what decision is justified, what evidence would change the conclusion and the smallest useful next action.

The numbers label the three passes of a repeated 33-perspective discipline. They do not imply 99 independently defined or validated tests. Preserve dissenting evidence; do not discard inconvenient facts in the name of distillation.

## 4. Application contract

For a consequential decision, record:
- the Point: question, purpose, decision and as-of date;
- the scope, exclusions and relevant stakeholders;
- known facts, assumptions, hypotheses and unknowns;
- the perspectives assessed and those deferred;
- source quality, independence, recency and limitations;
- counter-evidence and plausible alternative explanations;
- the decision owner and required ratification;
- the stop condition and next smallest useful action.

**No system design is complete merely because it has been discussed through multiple perspectives.** It is ready for a decision only when its material assumptions, risks, evidence gaps and authority boundaries are explicit. Implementation still requires the project's own gate.

## 5. Guardrails against misuse

- A high count of checked perspectives is not a quality score by itself.
- Do not average away a critical safeguarding, security or legal failure.
- Do not treat model output, search snippets or repeated copies of one source as independent corroboration.
- Do not confuse consensus with truth or confidence with certainty.
- Do not claim all 33 tiers are specified, validated or universally applicable.
- Record what would falsify the current conclusion.

## 6. Closing maxim

> Information without analysis is noise. Analysis without application is inertia. Engineering is the disciplined, evidence-bounded execution of what we have good reason to know.
