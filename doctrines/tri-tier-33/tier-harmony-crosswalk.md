# Tier ↔ Harmony Crosswalk

**Framework:** CG-TT33 v0.3.0-draft  
**Purpose:** Ensure every one of the 99 assessment tiers has at least one explicit relationship to the seven cross-layer Harmony invariants.

A mapping means the tier can contribute evidence to the invariant; it does not mean that a tier pass proves the invariant. The same tier may support multiple invariants. Project profiles may add mappings with rationale, but should not silently remove the baseline relationship.

## Use and maintenance

- A tier-to-invariant mapping is a review route, not a pass/fail shortcut.
- A Harmony probe should cite the relevant tier passports and evidence IDs.
- If a project-specific architecture makes a mapping inapplicable, record the rationale in its profile; retain the universal crosswalk unchanged unless the framework itself is revised.
- Revisit mappings when tier definitions or Harmony invariants change.
- A script may validate that all 99 tier IDs appear exactly once in this crosswalk and each has at least one H-1…H-7 mapping.
