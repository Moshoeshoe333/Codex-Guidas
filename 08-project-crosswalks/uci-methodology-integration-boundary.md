# UCI and Tri-Tier 33 Integration Boundary

**Status:** Proposed cross-project reference — informational, not an authority grant  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Primary governance reference:** [UCI Tri-Tier 33 Charter Integration and Methodology Governance](https://github.com/Moshoeshoe333/UCI-Foundation/blob/main/governance/tri-tier-33-charter-integration-and-methodology-governance.md)

## Purpose

This note records how Codex-Guidas relates to UCI and the two distinct 33 frameworks. It prevents reusable engineering knowledge from being confused with institutional authority, project implementation or certification.

## Boundary

- **Codex-Guidas** preserves reusable engineering doctrine, methods, lessons, defects, verification practices and cross-project knowledge. It is a knowledge base, not a command authority over projects.
- **UCI-Foundation** records UCI's proposed institutional mission, governance, research standards, service boundaries and institutional decisions. UCI's policies apply to UCI activities when properly approved; they do not automatically govern unrelated projects.
- **Tri-Tier 33** is the technical assessment instrument described in the Codex-Guidas doctrine. Its Mind / Body / Spirit dimensions and Harmony seams are distinct from UCI's strategic 33-Perspective framework.
- **UCI 33-Perspective framework** is a multidisciplinary decision-challenge method. It does not replace technical verification or the Tri-Tier 33 instrument.
- **Project repositories** remain the source of truth for their own implementation, tests, data, defects, release artifacts and project decisions.

## Transfer rule

A lesson may be proposed for reuse, but the receiving project must assess context, record adoption or rejection, adapt it, and verify it locally. Copying a rule is not proof of implementation. A passing test proves only the tested property under the recorded conditions.

## Evidence and authority

No framework score or document may:
- certify a project as secure or compliant;
- override project-specific safety, privacy, authorisation or release gates;
- authorise active testing, data collection, deployment or external commitments;
- convert an unverified proposal into a proven practice.

Use explicit evidence labels, exact source references, limitations and correction history. Unknown is not zero.

## Current status

This note is a proposed cross-reference. The UCI charter is not ratified, and this note does not adopt it as binding policy for Codex-Guidas or any other repository. Each project retains its own decision rights.

For detailed role definitions, non-compensable gates, knowledge transfer protocol and change control, consult the linked UCI charter.
