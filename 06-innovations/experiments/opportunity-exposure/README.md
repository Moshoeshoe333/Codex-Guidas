## Portfolio rules

1. Keep three independent experiment records. Do not collapse their results into one exposure score.
2. Record baseline before claiming improvement.
3. Count relevant exposure, qualified response, access, trust/readiness and outcome separately.
4. Do not invent response rates, market demand, user validation or commercial traction.
5. Preserve negative results, non-responses, cost and confounders.
6. Protect the user's time, finances, privacy and safety. Never pay to unlock a job, partner or speculative opportunity.
7. No project gate is changed by this portfolio.
8. ScoutSA follows its own owner-approved sequence: **Build → Test → Audit → Accept → Demonstrate → Pitch.**
9. Entrepreneurship remains a hypothesis until credible source evidence supports a specific problem and buyer.
10. Update the records with dates, source IDs, exact repository/test refs, and bounded conclusions.
