## 1. Question and hypothesis

**Question:** Can a focused, evidence-led, email-only job search across operations/procurement and operations-technology roles produce more qualified employer responses per suitable application than broad, untargeted searching?

**Hypothesis:** If each opportunity is screened against verified experience, essential requirements, location, salary expectations and a usable email application route before a tailored application is sent, then application quality and the rate of substantive responses should improve relative to indiscriminate applications.

This is a practical test, not a guarantee of employment and not proof that visibility alone creates access.

## 2. Candidate constraints to preserve

These constraints are carried from the candidate's previously supplied job-search preferences and must be re-confirmed only if the candidate changes them:

- Geography: Vaal Triangle and Johannesburg / wider Gauteng where commute or relocation is practical.
- Contact channel: **email only**; do not require or initiate phone calls.
- Expected gross monthly salary: **R12,000–R18,000** where salary is disclosed.
- Role families: operations, procurement, administration, logistics coordination, customer service, business support, systems/application support and suitable junior technology opportunities.
- Do not claim a qualification, certification, experience level or technical capability that is not supported by the current CV or portfolio evidence.
- Keep the candidate's right to review each application before it is sent.

## 3. Baseline and timebox

**Proposed window:** 14 calendar days, 2026-11-24 to 2026-12-08.  
**Baseline:** Not recorded. Before the first application, record the exact CV file/version, role-specific cover-letter version, LinkedIn/GitHub links used, existing active applications known to the candidate, and the date of the snapshot. Do not invent historical application counts or response rates.

**Pilot cap:** Screen up to 8 high-fit opportunities during the window. This is a workload ceiling, not a quota. Fewer well-matched applications are preferable to unsuitable volume.

## 4. Pre-registered screening rule

A listing may be promoted from lead to **qualified application candidate** only when all checks below are recorded:

1. The vacancy is still open on an identifiable employer or authorised recruiter source.
2. The full job description and essential requirements are accessible.
3. The candidate can evidence each essential requirement, or any gap is clearly identified before applying.
4. Location, work pattern and likely transport/relocation burden are practical.
5. Salary is within the preferred range, or is undisclosed and can be clarified through an email route before substantial commitment. Do not assume an undisclosed salary fits.
6. The application can be completed without a phone call or mandatory phone number. If phone contact is mandatory, hold or exclude it.
7. A legitimate email application route or employer-controlled application process is verified.
8. The submitted CV and cover letter contain only accurate, current claims.

Do not apply from an aggregator snippet alone. Verify the listing with the employer/recruiter source first.

## 7. Decision criteria

**Process integrity:** every submitted application must pass all pre-registered screening rules, contain accurate claims, and have a recorded source and submission date.

**Positive outcome signal:** a substantive response tied to an application, such as an interview invitation, skills assessment, recruiter request for evidence or a clear next step. Generic acknowledgements and automated receipts are tracked separately.

**Interpretation:** a 14-day period may be too short to observe hiring outcomes. No response does not prove the CV is poor or the candidate is unsuitable; record timing, seasonality, role fit and response lag as limitations.

**Decision:** Continue / Change / Stop / Defer after the window, based on verified applications, responses, effort and quality of the available opportunities.

## 8. Stop and safety rules

- No phone calls or phone-first application processes.
- No application fees, recruitment deposits, paid placement promises or requests for OTPs, banking passwords, identity credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.
- Verify the employer and application destination before uploading a CV.
- Do not publish private contact information in public portfolios.
- Do not inflate experience, qualifications or project status to improve exposure.
- Candidate review is required before each email/application is sent.
