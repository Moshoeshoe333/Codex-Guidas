# Pack: Point → Knowing → Witness → Gate

**Pack ID:** CG-PACK-001

**Upstream source:** 02-methodologies/33-tier-framework.md and doctrines/33-tier-framework.md

## Purpose
A compact discipline for keeping purpose, knowledge, evidence and authority distinct.

## The sequence
1. **Point — Why?** Define the problem, intended outcome, affected people, decision and scope.
2. **Knowing — What do we know?** Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, hypotheses and unknowns.
3. **Witness — What supports the claim?** Preserve inspectable evidence, provenance, dates, methods, limitations and counter-evidence.
4. **Gate — Who/what authorizes the next step?** Define entry/exit criteria, decision authority, unresolved blockers and the smallest safe next action.

## Operating rules
- A design is not an implementation.
- An implementation is not proof of correct operation.
- A passing test proves only what it exercised under recorded conditions.
- Evidence does not automatically confer authority.
- Unknowns remain visible until resolved.
- Contradictions are recorded, not hidden.
- A gate is a decision boundary, not a ceremony.
- The strength of a claim must never exceed the strength of its evidence.

## Adaptation
Use for design reviews, research, incidents, release decisions, procurement, operational changes and new project formation. Scale the record to consequence and reversibility. Do not force a full review on routine low-risk changes.

## Non-claims
This sequence is not a certification, legal standard, risk score or replacement for technical/domain review. The related 33-tier and 33-perspective materials have their own maturity boundaries; do not claim every tier or lens is fully specified or validated.
