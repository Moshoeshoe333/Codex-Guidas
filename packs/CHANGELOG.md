## Change discipline

- Do not change a pack's version or maturity merely because its documentation was edited.
- A maturity change requires an explicit decision and evidence; it is not implied by a passing repository validator.
- Preserve local adoption records and adaptations. Upstream changes must never silently overwrite downstream copies.
- A changelog entry does not grant reuse rights. Consult the repository's licensing decision and applicable ownership before external redistribution.
