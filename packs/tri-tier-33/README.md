## What this pack contains

The complete portable pack is the seven-file directory at **doctrines/tri-tier-33/**:
- README.md — architecture, governance, proportionality and adoption.
- codebase-33.md — Mind / Codebase 33.
- persistence-33.md — Body / Persistence 33.
- ui-33.md — Spirit / UI 33.
- tripartite-harmony.md — seven invariants, probe contracts and scoring.
- evidence-and-passport.md — evidence model, verdicts and passport template.
- tier-harmony-crosswalk.md — explicit mapping of all 99 tiers to H-1…H-7.

To adopt, copy the entire canonical directory into the target repository (for example, docs/methods/tri-tier-33/) and add the standard adoption record. Do not copy only this catalogue page.

## What changed in v0.3.0-draft

- Kept the original 33 + 33 + 33 structure and Circle A/B/C organization.
- Added Applicable / Not applicable / Not assessed dispositions so 99 manual essays are not compulsory.
- Defined SOLID / SOFT / OPEN / GHOST with freshness and provenance boundaries.
- Clarified that L0–L6 is claim-specific, not a universal ranking of evidence strength.
- Refined Harmony for version compatibility, logical atomicity, retries, interruption, scoped authority and traceable disagreements.
- Replaced median-only confidence with per-invariant results, coverage, lowest seam, median seam and non-compensable blockers.
- Made backup/export, offline semantics, telemetry, raw record access and domain-specific patterns conditional on real requirements and risk.
- Separated the universal technical core from project-specific profiles.

## Relationship to other Codex-Guidas methods

- Point → Knowing → Witness → Gate governs reasoning and authorization.
- The 33-Perspective Master Register reviews broader human, strategic, operational, legal and commercial realities.
- Tri-Tier 33 reviews technical integrity across code, persistence and UI.

Do not combine their scores or treat one as approval from another.

## Adoption boundary

This pack does not certify security, privacy, legal compliance, safeguarding, production readiness or market demand. It does not authorize implementation. Project owners must define local profiles, risk triggers, acceptance criteria and approval gates. Preserve the upstream version/commit and revalidate every adopted control in the target project.
