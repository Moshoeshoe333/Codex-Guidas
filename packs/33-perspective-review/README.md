# Pack: 33-Perspective Review

**Pack ID:** CG-PACK-003  
**Version:** 0.2.0  
**Maturity:** Proposed working register; not ratified as a canonical standard.  
**Upstream source:** doctrines/33-perspective-master-register.md

## Purpose
Challenge consequential decisions from multiple angles, surface missing evidence and identify non-compensable blockers.

## Use
- Select a review depth proportionate to consequence and reversibility.
- Assess relevant perspectives as Pass / Concern / Fail / Not assessed / Not applicable.
- Attach a short evidence reference and rationale to every consequential assessment.
- Treat Not assessed as an evidence gap, never as a pass.
- Do not average away critical safeguarding, privacy, security, legal or data-integrity failures.
- Do not assign numeric weights without empirical validation and a defensible model.

## Review depth
- **L0 Routine:** relevant lenses only; low-risk reversible maintenance.
- **L1 Material:** affected users, data flows, dependencies and rollback.
- **L2 High consequence:** explicit critical-lens review, independent challenge and named approval.
- **L3 Strategic/irreversible:** broad review, counter-evidence, staged validation and formal gate.

These levels are proposed guidance, not calibrated risk thresholds.

## The 33 lenses
The current upstream register covers purpose, dignity, stakeholders, accessibility, language, safeguarding, privacy, security, law, consent/authority, provenance/freshness, evidence/uncertainty, correctness, failure modes, resilience, offline grace, device constraints, bandwidth, cost, maintainability/dependency decay, handover, testability/observability, performance, interoperability, supply-chain risk, portability, usability/entry friction, operations, reversibility, sustainability, demand, outcomes and long-term viability.

Use the upstream register for the full questions, evidence sought, failure signals and verification approaches. This pack does not duplicate that evolving table; record the exact upstream version/commit when adopting it.

## Limitations
Version 0.2 reflects three project desk reviews and five refinements. Desk reviews are not empirical validation. The register remains proposed pending real-decision testing, specialist challenge and explicit ratification. A completed review does not authorize implementation.
